The creative mind behind Shaadi Reels

Meet Mukul Saraogi

A digital creative professional and visual storyteller whose journey began in graphic design and evolved through event management, technical direction, animation and photography — the craft in which he found his true calling in 2009.

As Assistant Director of Photography and Photographer, he has worked across 21 Bollywood films and 2,500+ fashion and advertising shoots. In 2010 he founded Mukkz Studio, expanded into wedding photography in 2014 — and, keeping with the times, launched Shaadi Reels in April 2025, now in Delhi and Dubai.
